## 🛒 M.JayaEswar Ventures — Founder *(2026 – Present)*

> I founded **M.JayaEswar Ventures**, the parent brand for four online brands that sell across Europe in euros. I chose the platforms, named the brands, connected the suppliers, built the catalogues and storefronts, and audited every store as a customer before go-live — the same supply-chain, sales and data discipline from my earlier roles, now applied to a business I own end to end.

## ✅ What I have built — Stage 1 *(complete, Oct 2026)*

| Area | What I did | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 🏷️ **Brand architecture** | One parent brand and four store brands; replaced names that were not unique (e.g. a taken .com) | 1 parent · 4 distinct brands |
| 🌍 **Market & pricing** | Moved every store to all of Europe with EUR as the default currency | One market, one currency |
| 🌐 **Domain** | Bought and connected **enjoyvo.com** to the flagship | Branded URL |
| 📦 **Catalogue & suppliers** | Supplier apps first, IndiaMart / Alibaba as second source, Printful for print-on-demand; raw / demo imports kept off the shelf | **156 live products** across **10 categories** (150+ trust target met) |
| 🔍 **Catalogue QA** | Crawled every flagship product's options; logged single-value options and mislabelled variants with product IDs | Clean-up list ready |
| 🛡️ **Trust layer** | Trust blocks, "all in one place" banner, real contact page, secure-checkout block, rebuilt footer (14 working links), template brand names removed | No placeholder content |
| 🤖 **Anti-spam** | Captcha on register, reviews, contact and returns; bot account removed | New bot sign-ups blocked |
| 🧪 **Customer-eye audit** | Anonymous mystery-shopper run across all 4 stores + the group site — home, product, cart, checkout, policies, search, mobile | Go-live verdict per store |
